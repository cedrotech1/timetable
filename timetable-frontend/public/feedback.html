Frequently Asked Questions

The e-ticket system is University of Rwanda’s online helpdesk. Submit a support request about Hostels, Finance, Assets, or Other topics. Each submission becomes a ticket that staff track and reply to until it is resolved.

The form has four sections:
1. Identification & contact — name, email, phone, requestor type, and extra details (registration number, campus, college) depending on who you are.
2. Request details — request category, subject, and a summary preview of what will be sent.
3. Your message — describe your issue in your own words.
4. Attachments — optional files (you can add more than one).
Fields marked with a red * are required — the form will not submit until they are filled in.

Choose your Requestor category:
Student — registration number, campus, and college are all required.
Staff — campus and college are required.
Alumni — registration number, campus, and college are optional.
External Stakeholder / Partner — no registration number, campus, or college fields.
Campus and college are selected from UR dropdown lists (Busogo, Gikondo, Huye, Nyagatare, Nyarugenge, Remera, Rukara, Rwamagana, and UR colleges).

Pick a Request category first, then a Subject:
Hostels — cleaning, found material, repair and maintenance, toilet paper.
Finance — accommodation fees, tuition fees, retake fees.
Assets — water, electricity, repair.
Other — type your own subject (no dropdown).
Category and subject are sent with your ticket automatically; you only write the detailed message in the message box.

Email is the main way to follow up. Use the same email you entered on the form.
After submit, you should receive a confirmation email with your ticket reference.
When staff reply, you get an email notification — reply to that email to continue the same ticket.
You can also sign in at eticket.ur.ac.rw to view tickets online.

Yes. Attachments are optional. Start with one file, then click + Add another file if you need to upload more (up to five files). Use PDF, images, or documents that help explain your request.

We aim to respond within 24 hours on working days (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM CAT). Complex cases may take longer. For urgent matters, call during working hours or mention “urgent” in your subject.
